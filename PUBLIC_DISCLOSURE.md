# Public Disclosure & Content Boundaries

FactoryMind is presented publicly as a technology demonstration of multilingual, citation-grounded RAG.

## Demo corpus

The current demo uses diving training manuals as a real-world document corpus.

The public interface does **not** provide direct download access to the full source PDFs. It displays only limited source excerpts needed to verify an answer.

Names, manuals, trademarks, and other intellectual property remain the property of their respective owners.

The public demo is independent and is **not** the official IANTD website.

## Safety framing

The diving demo is an educational/reference demonstration.

It is not a substitute for:

- certified instruction
- dive planning
- medical advice
- emergency procedures
- professional judgment

## Visual styling

The red `CLASSIFIED` stamp and redaction-inspired presentation are visual design elements only. They do not mean that the source material is actually classified or restricted government information.

## Repository scope

This repository is intentionally a case study rather than an open-source release.

It describes:

- product goals
- behavior
- architecture at a high level
- deployment characteristics
- public safeguards

It does not disclose the proprietary implementation.
